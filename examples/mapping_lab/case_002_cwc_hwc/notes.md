# Case 002 - CWC + HWC

Sanitized mock case for a water-coil ERV submittal where the cover page includes one chilled water cooling coil and one hot water heating coil.

## Intended Mapping Lessons

- `CCWC-1` maps to Direct Coil chilled water layout.
- `HHWC-1` maps to Direct Coil hot water layout, not condensing.
- `Cooling CWC` and `Heating HWC` sections must remain separate.
- Fluid fields map to Direct Coil fluid data, not refrigerant data.
