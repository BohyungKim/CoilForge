1 · EZ Template Configurations 22 buckets · (category, hand, header, special)

Each bucket is one drawing configuration. Only buckets seeded from a provided EZ PDF generate a drawing; mirror-derived hands are forbidden (John, 2026-06-17), so every hand needs its own seed. Unregistered buckets split by why: a known reference exists but isn't seeded, a hand-pair isn't seeded, or no reference drawing exists at all.

3 · Template Field (Slot) Mapping 50 slots scanned across 10 active templates

Every {{slot.X}} in the templates must classify into exactly one bucket — a CI gate fails on any UNCLASSIFIED slot. Logic-derived slots come from the rule engine or recovered formulas; material/title slots come from the draft or EZ JSON; the rest are intentionally REVIEW REQUIRED (mass-properties & secondary materials that no rule can derive). UNCLASSIFIED = 0 — nothing is silently unmapped.
